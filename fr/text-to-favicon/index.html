What's Included in Your Download

Every file you need for full cross-browser and cross-device favicon support

ICO

favicon.ico

The classic multi-size ICO format, containing 16×16, 32×32, and 48×48 icons. Required for legacy browser support including older versions of Internet Explorer.

16

favicon-16x16.png

Standard browser tab icon at 16×16 pixels. Used by most modern browsers as the primary tab favicon for standard-density displays.

32

favicon-32x32.png

High-DPI browser tab icon at 32×32 pixels. Displayed on Retina and HiDPI screens for crisp, sharp favicon rendering in browser tabs.

180

apple-touch-icon.png

Apple Touch Icon at 180×180 pixels for iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch. Displayed when users add your website to their iOS home screen.

192

android-chrome-192x192.png

Android home screen icon at 192×192 pixels. Used when Android users add your site to their home screen via Chrome or other browsers.

512

android-chrome-512x512.png

High-resolution PWA icon at 512×512 pixels. Required for Progressive Web App install prompts and splash screens on Android devices.

JSON

site.webmanifest

Fichier manifeste d'application Web contenant des références d'icônes, la couleur du thème et la couleur d'arrière-plan. Essentiel pour la prise en charge des PWA et l'intégration à l'écran d'accueil Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use a text favicon instead of an image?
Text favicons are perfect for brands in their early stages, personal portfolios, blogs, and clean minimalist websites. Initials are easy to identify in a crowded browser tab, enhancing brand recognition without requiring complex graphic design.

What font looks best for a favicon?
Bold, thick sans-serif fonts such as Space Grotesk, Montserrat, and Outfit are highly recommended. Monospaced styles also look excellent. Try to avoid extremely thin fonts, as they become unreadable when scaled down to 16×16 pixels.

Is my data private on this website?
Yes, 100%. The generator runs entirely within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your text, custom designs, and generated files are never uploaded to our servers, keeping your brand identity private.

What file formats does this tool output?
Our text favicon generator outputs a standard multi-size `favicon.ico` (which packs 16px, 32px, and 48px sizes), multiple high-resolution browser-tab PNGs, Apple Touch Icons (180x180), Android Chrome icons (192x192, 512x512), and a `site.webmanifest` configuration file.
